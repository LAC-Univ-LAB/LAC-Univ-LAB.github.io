Prospective Students

LAC Analysis & Behavior Lab. (LAB) is actively looking for motivated students who are interested in conducting research with us.

LAB welcomes students from diverse backgrounds and research interests. We encourage ambitious and unconventional research ideas, particularly those involving artificial intelligence, human behavior, intelligent systems, and real-world problems in Los Angeles.

How to Apply

If you are interested in joining LAB, please contact Prof. Tommy through one of the following methods:

  • Discord: Send a DM or friend request to .___tommy___.
  • LAC In-Game: Call 213-3192-0678
  • E-mail: Send your CV to LacUniv.Lab@gmail.com

When contacting us by e-mail, please include a brief introduction, your research interests, and your CV.

Applicants do not need to have a fully developed research topic. Students with interesting questions, unusual ideas, or a strong willingness to conduct research are encouraged to apply.
