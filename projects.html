Projects

Constructing a Descriptive Model of Voyeuristic Behavior (DMV) via Ground


This study aims to qualitatively analyze the behavioral and psychological characteristics of crowds gathering near the central Emergency Medical Services (EMS) facility in a metropolitan area and, based on the findings, to develop a Descriptive Model of Voyeuristic Behavior (DMV).

Risk–Reward Analysis of Criminal Activities in Los Santos


We develop a data-driven framework that quantifies the risk and expected reward of diverse criminal activities in Los Santos by integrating multi-source data, predictive modeling, and probabilistic outcome estimation. The system supports strategic decision-making through interpretable risk–reward scores under dynamic environmental conditions.
