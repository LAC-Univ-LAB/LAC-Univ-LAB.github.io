Welcome!

Welcome to the LAC Analysis & Behavior Lab. (LAB) at LAC Univ. Our lab studies behavioral, social, and urban phenomena in Los Angeles through data-driven analysis, artificial intelligence, and computational modeling.

We are particularly interested in understanding how individuals and organizations make decisions within complex urban environments. Our research spans a broad range of topics, including human behavior, economic activity, public safety, mobility, risk analysis, and intelligent decision-making.

Rather than limiting ourselves to a single research discipline, LAB encourages research driven by interesting questions found throughout Los Angeles. We combine quantitative analysis, machine learning, behavioral modeling, and field observations to transform everyday phenomena into meaningful research.

About Tommy:

Prof. Tommy, Ph.D. is a Professor at LAC Univ. and the founder and director of the LAC Analysis & Behavior Lab. (LAB). Her research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, explainable AI, robotics, human behavior, and decision intelligence, with a particular interest in developing intelligent systems that can understand and reason about complex real-world environments.

Throughout her research career, she has worked across a broad spectrum of AI, ranging from computer vision and multimodal learning to autonomous systems, behavioral modeling, and urban intelligence. Rather than focusing solely on theoretical performance, her research emphasizes AI systems that can operate in the real world, explain their decisions, and provide meaningful insights into human and societal behavior.

At LAC Univ., Prof. Tommy established LAB as an interdisciplinary research group where artificial intelligence meets the dynamics of Los Angeles. Under her direction, the lab investigates unconventional but fundamental questions about the city: how people make decisions, how organizations and markets behave, how risk influences human activity, and how complex patterns emerge from interactions between individuals, technology, and urban systems.

Her work has resulted in numerous research publications spanning artificial intelligence, computer vision, robotics, intelligent systems, and data-driven decision making. She has also contributed to the international research community through conference reviewing, academic service, collaborative research, and the mentoring of young researchers.

Beyond her own research, Prof. Tommy is committed to building an environment where researchers are encouraged to pursue ambitious and unconventional ideas. At LAB, students are expected not simply to follow established research directions, but to discover important questions of their own and develop the methods needed to answer them.

Her research philosophy forms the foundation of LAB: observe carefully, analyze rigorously, understand deeply, and go beyond.
